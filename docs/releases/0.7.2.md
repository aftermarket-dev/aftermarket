Aftermarket 0.7.2 tightens how patch, adapt and record handle request bodies that don't arrive the normal way: slowly, not at all, cut off, or after the upstream has already answered.

- **A stalled upload is let go.** Every part of a request body must arrive within `upstream_timeout` of the last, whether a rule reads the body or it's streamed through. A client that stops gets `408` and its connection is closed. An upload that is slow but keeps moving is never cut off. Before, a client that stalled while the upstream was down could hold the 502 back for good.
- **A cut-off upload is never passed on as whole.** A body that breaks off before its announced length gets `400`. A rule, a template or a recording never treats the part that arrived as the whole body.
- **Early answers close the connection.** When the upstream answers before the client has sent its whole body, the answer arrives whole and closes the connection, so nothing the client sends after it can be read as a new request.
- **Half-closed clients get their answer.** A client that closes its sending side after its request used to get an empty 200. It now gets the real answer, if the whole answer arrives within `upstream_timeout`. A client that really goes away frees the upstream call by then.
- **Keep-alive holds.** Ordinary traffic keeps its connection as before. When adapt replaces or drops a body, it now reads the old one off so the connection can be used again.
- **Exact error answers.** 502, 504, 408 and 400 answers carry a Content-Length. Client faults are logged as `upstream_error` with a reason that says the client's body stalled or broke off.

Four rounds of independent review went into this release. Every problem they found has a test that fails without its fix: stalled and crawling uploads, early answers with and without `Expect: 100-continue`, bodies just over `max_body`, half-closed clients, clients that leave mid-stream, a switch of protocols, cut-off bodies and keep-alive under load.

Recorded results are in `test/results`.

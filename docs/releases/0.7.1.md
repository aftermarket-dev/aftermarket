Aftermarket 0.7.1 fixes a bug that could cut off answers in patch, adapt and record, and closes two smaller gaps the fix turned up.

- **Answers cut off under load.** When a client sent a request with a body and the upstream answered quickly, Go's server could close that body while the proxy was still finishing it, and Go's transport then dropped the upstream connection in the middle of the answer. The client saw a short response and an "unexpected EOF". It happened rarely, more often on busy machines, and it was the occasional failure in the random pass-through tests on CI. The proxies now leave the request body to the transport until the answer has gone out. AfterGuard could hit the same thing when the app answered without reading the whole body, and is fixed the same way.
- **A stalled body after the 408.** When a client's body stopped arriving, patch and adapt answered 408 as before, but then kept waiting on that client. They now let go of the connection at once.

New tests fail without each fix: an upstream that answers after reading all, half or none of the body, a health check sent with a body on a reused connection, and a stalled body that must have its connection closed after the 408. The random pass-through tests ran 390 times under heavy load with no failure.

Recorded results are in `test/results`.
